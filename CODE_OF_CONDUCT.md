# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to make the outcomes of UK public inquiries more visible and accountable. The people affected by these inquiries — survivors, families, communities — deserve a project that handles their experiences with care and respect.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful environment for everyone who contributes, regardless of background, experience, or perspective.

---

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Focus on the data and the evidence, not on individuals or political positions
- Accept that decisions about evidence quality and data accuracy rest with the maintainer
- Acknowledge that contributors may have personal connections to these inquiries — treat all participation with appropriate sensitivity

---

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks in any form
- Submitting fabricated or misleading evidence
- Attempting to alter recommendation text for political or other purposes
- Attempting to use the contribution process to promote a particular political position
- Flooding the issue tracker to obstruct normal operation

---

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to close issues, reject pull requests, and block accounts that violate this code of conduct without further discussion.

---

## Reporting

If you experience behaviour that violates this code of conduct, please open a private issue or contact the maintainer directly via GitHub.

---
